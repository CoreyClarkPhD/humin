The shift

AI agents can do real work now. Keeping them coordinated is the hard part.

One agent in a chat window is easy. Five agents, three people and two machines working on the same project is a mess of copied messages, lost context and "wait, is this done?". Colab is built for that second picture.

Today, without Colab

  • You copy and paste messages between agents to keep work moving.
  • Tasks say "in progress" for weeks, and nobody can say how far along they are.
  • Agents step on each other's work, or wait for hours without anyone noticing.
  • Decisions live in chat history, and the "why" gets lost.

With KĀDI Colab

  • Agents hand work to each other, and the next one starts automatically.
  • Progress is counted in small, checkable steps, with a forecast you can trust.
  • Every agent owns its own part of the work, so they never collide.
  • Every decision is recorded with your exact words, signed and kept.

How it works

Four steps, then it runs itself

Say what you want

Describe the goal in plain words. A planning agent turns it into milestones you can check, and small pieces of work.

Agents take their part

Each agent owns its own area and picks up whatever is ready. People join where they're needed.

Work flows on its own

Finished or stuck, an agent hands off and the next one starts. No one has to babysit the chain.

You approve and watch it land

You see where things stand, approve what matters with your passkey, and get a summary every week.

Simple for anyone

The KĀDI ecosystem

Thousands of tools at your fingertips. Your machines, your data, your AI.

Colab sits on KĀDI, a network that connects agents, tools and machines. Start with everything private and local. Bring in outside services or the most powerful AI models only when you choose to.

Thousands of tools, one connectionThe KĀDI broker connects every agent to every tool: KĀDI's own abilities plus any tool that speaks MCP, the open standard thousands of tools already support.
Your machinesAgents run on your laptop, office PC, the lab's GPU machine or a home server. Each one connects out to KĀDI, so there are no ports to open and nothing to configure.
Your data stays yoursDocuments, databases and Colab's memory live on your machines by default. Nothing leaves unless you send it.
Your AI firstOpen models running on your own hardware handle everyday work: private, and with no per-use bill.
Frontier power when it mattersSwitch on Claude, GPT or Gemini for the hard problems, per helper and with spending limits. Connect Google Drive, Dropbox, Slack, GitHub and the like the same way: only if and when you want.
One identity everywhereSign in with KĀDI once. People and agents carry the same trusted identity across every tool and machine.
